# QueerCade

A hand-curated catalog of video games with LGBTQ+ characters and stories — every game reviewed by an editor, with details from IGDB and curated lists written by people.

## What you can do here

- **Discover** games: search by title or LGBTQ+ character name at `/discover`
- **Browse** the full library A to Z at `/library`
- **Explore collections**: themed lists like queer visual novels and cozy queer games at `/collections`
- **Use the API**: free, read-only, no key at `/developers`

## For AI agents

- `llms.txt`: agent guide to this site
- MCP server: `https://queercade.mikedemo.dev/api/public/mcp` — tools `search_games`, `get_game`, `list_collections`, `get_collection`, `list_facets`
- REST API: `https://queercade.mikedemo.dev/api/public/v1` (spec at `/openapi.json`)
- Agent skills: `/.well-known/agent-skills/`
- Everything is free and read-only; no authentication required.
